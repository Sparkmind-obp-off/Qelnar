# QELNAR One — Customer Segments & Jobs to Be Done

Version 1.0 · Hypotheses to validate · 2026-11-14

> This is a product hypothesis, not completed customer discovery. Validate with interviews and observed workflows before treating priorities as proven demand.

## Primary: independent creator-operator
Runs one or several channels, creates and reviews performance personally, and relies on platform analytics, spreadsheets, notes and AI tools.
- Understand which content and topics work.
- Find repeatable formats and audience questions.
- Know what to publish or test next.
- Reduce time spent collecting and comparing data.
- Keep a reliable record of experiments and results.

## Secondary: faceless / niche content operator
Runs themed channels and tests formats, topics, hooks and publishing cadences.
- Discover topics and formats with evidence.
- Compare competing channels and public content.
- Turn findings into briefs, scripts and production tasks.
- Reuse learnings without losing source context.

## Secondary: creator-led small business
Uses content to support leads, sales, community or brand awareness.
- Connect content activity to business goals.
- Coordinate approvals and responsibilities.
- Understand which content contributes to qualified attention or conversions.
- Keep a reviewable history of actions and outcomes.

## Later: small creator team / agency
Manages multiple creators, clients or brands.
- Separate client data and credentials.
- Assign work to people and agents.
- Produce repeatable reports and playbooks.
- Audit who approved and executed each action.

## Jobs-to-be-done hierarchy

| Job | User question | QELNAR capability |
|---|---|---|
| Orient | What is happening? | Unified overview and source health |
| Diagnose | Why might it be happening? | Evidence-linked analysis |
| Discover | What is changing in my niche? | Trend and competitor intelligence |
| Decide | What should I do next? | Prioritized recommendations |
| Coordinate | Who or what should do it? | Plans, tasks and specialist agents |
| Control | What happens if I approve? | Approval preview and policy |
| Learn | Did it work? | Outcome tracking and experiment history |

## Highest-value initial problem
“Which recent content pieces and topics deserve attention, what evidence supports that conclusion, and what is one sensible next experiment?”

This is narrow enough to implement and meaningful enough to test recurring value.

## Customer discovery plan
Conduct 8–12 structured interviews across the initial segments. Ask participants to demonstrate their last content review instead of describing an ideal future product. Capture:
- Channels and tools currently used.
- Time spent collecting and interpreting metrics.
- Most recent decision made from analytics.
- How topics and competitors are selected.
- What they automate and what they refuse to automate.
- Trust requirements for AI recommendations.
- Budget and willingness-to-pay signals.
- Data they would connect and permissions they would grant.

Do not lead with a product pitch. Record exact phrases, workflow evidence, frequency and current workaround.

## Validation gates
- Problem evidence: at least 6 of 10 interviewees independently describe the same costly workflow.
- Prototype evidence: at least 5 users complete the first insight workflow without facilitator intervention.
- Repeat value: users return to review evidence or outcomes in a second week.
- Trust evidence: users can explain why an insight exists and what permission an action needs.
- Commercial evidence: obtain explicit willingness-to-pay evidence before adding paid provider infrastructure.

## Business-model validation

The first customer interviews must distinguish demand for:
- A self-serve intelligence SaaS.
- A recurring research/reporting service.
- A hybrid product with assisted setup.

Do not infer that users want an agency or managed-service model merely because the early product needs manual onboarding. Record willingness to pay separately for software access and optional human assistance. See `SERVICE_VS_SAAS_PRODUCT_MODEL.md`.
